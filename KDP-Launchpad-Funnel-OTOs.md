# KDP Launchpad — Sales Funnel & OTO Plan

WarriorPlus / JVZoo / LaunchpadJV ke liye complete funnel structure.
Yeh structure market-tested pattern (KDP Master, BookMint, KidsKDPAI jaise launches) par based hai.

---

## Front End (FE) — KDP Launchpad Commercial

- **Price:** $17 (launch), baad me $27
- **Kya milega:**
  - Core app access (limited/commercial license)
  - Niche research (limited — e.g. 10/month)
  - Cover generation (limited)
  - Ebook generation (limited)
  - Basic title optimizer
- **Maksad:** Sasta entry point taaki max buyers andar aayein, phir OTOs se profit.

---

## OTO 1 — Unlimited Edition

- **Price:** $37
- **Downsell:** $27 (same features, "lite unlimited" ya installment framing)
- **Kya milega:**
  - Unlimited books
  - Unlimited niche research
  - Unlimited cover generation
  - Unlimited ebook generation
  - Saare monthly caps hata diye jaate hain
- **Kyun:** Har funnel ka #1 best-converting upsell. FE me limits jaan-bujhkar rakhi jaati hain taaki yahan unlock bike.

---

## OTO 2 — Done-For-You (DFY) Edition

- **Price:** $67
- **Downsell:** $47 (kam books, e.g. 50 ke jagah 25)
- **Kya milega:**
  - 50–100 ready-made / pre-built books
  - Done-for-you profitable niches (pehle se research ki hui)
  - Pre-made cover templates
  - Ready-to-publish ebook + storybook templates
  - Buyer ko zero kaam — bas publish karo
- **Kyun:** Beginners "shortcut" ke liye pay karte hain. High perceived value.

---

## OTO 3 — Autopilot / Automation Edition

- **Price:** $97
- **Downsell:** $67 (limited automation runs / month)
- **Kya milega:**
  - Full Autopilot feature: auto niche → auto book → auto cover → auto publish package
  - Bulk generation (ek saath multiple books)
  - Scheduling (set-and-forget)
  - Queue management
- **Kyun:** "Hands-free income" angle — sabse strong emotional hook, high ticket justify karta hai.

---

## OTO 4 — Bundle & Series Empire

- **Price:** $47
- **Downsell:** $37
- **Kya milega:**
  - Bundle feature unlock
  - Book bundles / box-sets banane ki power
  - Ek book se poori series create karna
  - Multi-book publish package
- **Kyun:** Buyer ki khud ki AOV badhti hai (ek book se kai products) — value clear hai.

---

## OTO 5 — Agency / Client Edition

- **Price:** $97
- **Downsell:** $67 (kam client seats)
- **Kya milega:**
  - Client / sub-user accounts (e.g. 50 clients)
  - Client dashboard
  - Agency license — dusron ke liye books banakar bech sakein
  - White-label reports
  - Client management tools
- **Kyun:** "Business banao" angle — users clients se paisa kama sakte hain, isliye high price accept karte hain.

---

## OTO 6 — Reseller / Whitelabel Edition

- **Price:** $197
- **Downsell:** $97 (kam license slots, e.g. 250 ke jagah 100)
- **Kya milega:**
  - Apne brand naam se KDP Launchpad bechne ka right
  - 100–250 license slots
  - Tera taiyaar kiya hua sales material (sales page, emails)
  - Support tera team handle karega unke customers ke liye
- **Kyun:** Highest-ticket, funnel ka last aur sabse profitable step. Serious buyers hi lete hain.

## Zaroori Tips

- **Har OTO ka downsell zaroor rakh** — jo "No" bole usko sasti/limited version offer karke bhi capture karo.
- **Order matter karta hai:** Unlimited pehle (sabse zyada convert), Reseller last (sabse mehenga).
- **Ek "Bundle Deal" bhi rakh** — FE + saare OTOs ek discounted price pe (e.g. $247). Kai buyers pura funnel ek saath le lete hain.
- **Affiliates ko 50% commission** de pura funnel pe — WarriorPlus/JVZoo ka standard, promotion badhata hai.
- **6 OTO se zyada mat badha** — refund/chargeback risk kam rehta hai aur buyer overwhelm nahi hota.
