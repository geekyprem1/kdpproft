## Validation checklist (use before every niche)

Ask:

1. Can I explain the book in one sentence?  
2. Would someone gift this?  
3. Is the niche specific enough for a cover to make sense?  
4. Am I avoiding trademarks / celebrities / brand IP?  
5. Does KDP Profit Machine support this book type for me right now?

If you get 4–5 yes answers → generate.

## Niches to avoid (beginner traps)

- Anything with Disney / Marvel / Pokemon / celebrity names  
- “Make money online” puzzle books with scammy claims  
- Overly broad: Love, Life, Happy, Awesome  
- Controversial political niches on day one  
- Medical claim journals (“cure anxiety”) — keep wellness gentle and honest  
- Exact copies of famous book titles

## Appendix — Niche research lite (no tools required)

Before generating, spend 5 minutes on Amazon:

1. Search your niche + book type (`cats word search`)  
2. Open top 5 results  
3. Note common title patterns  
4. Note if “large print”, “for kids”, “answer key” appear often  
5. Make your title clear and competitive — not copied  

Then generate your original book in **KDP Profit Machine**.

### Red flags on Amazon search
- Results are dominated by one mega-brand IP → pick a different angle  
- Every cover looks identical and ranks with thousands of reviews → go more specific  
- Niche is so dead nothing relevant appears → pick another from this sheet  

### Green flags
- Multiple books selling in the format  
- Clear gift language in titles  
- Room for a cleaner cover / clearer promise
