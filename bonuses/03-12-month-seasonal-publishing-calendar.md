# BONUS #3 — 12-Month Seasonal Publishing Calendar

**Product:** KDP Mafia  
**Perceived value:** $27  
**Core rule:** Publish seasonal books **6–8 weeks before** peak buying  
**Goal:** Never scramble last-minute for Halloween/Christmas again

---

## Why seasonal books print money

Evergreen books pay rent.  
Seasonal books create **spikes**.

Parents, gift buyers, and teachers search with urgency:

- “Halloween coloring book for kids”
- “Christmas activity book”
- “Mother’s Day journal”

If your book is live early, you catch:

1. Early planners  
2. Peak gift rush  
3. Next-year leftover traffic  

If you publish Dec 20 for Christmas, you missed the war.

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|

## January — New Year & fresh starts

### Buyer psychology
People want reset, habits, health-ish goals, cozy winter puzzles.

### Best book types
Habit Tracker · Gratitude Journal · Easy Sudoku · Word Search · Goal Planner

### Niche ideas
1. 90-Day Habit Tracker  
2. New Year Goals Journal  
3. Winter Word Search for Adults  
4. “New Year Same Me” funny puzzle book  
5. Dry-ish wellness journal (no medical claims)  
6. Budget tracker / simple money log  
7. Hot cocoa / winter cozy coloring for kids  

### Title angles
- 90-Day Habit Tracker — Undated Daily Layout  
- New Year Goals Journal — Plan, Track, Reflect  
- Winter Word Search Puzzle Book for Adults  

### Publish timing
- Best: early December  
- Acceptable: first week of January  
- Late: after Jan 10 (still okay for habits, weaker for “New Year” branding)

### Content notes
Keep wellness honest. No “cure” language.  
Undated trackers sell longer than dated ones.

## Seasonal keyword add-ons (use with Bonus #4)

- halloween coloring book kids  
- christmas activity book  
- mothers day gift journal  
- back to school activity book  
- thanksgiving gratitude journal  
- valentines day coloring book  

Keep other slots evergreen so the book can sell after the holiday too.

---

## Cover rules for seasonal books

1. **Holiday must be obvious at thumbnail size**  
2. Use classic symbols (tree, pumpkin, heart, egg) — not tiny details  
3. Cute usually beats scary for kids volume  
4. Don’t overload with 12 icons  
5. Title should include the holiday OR the season word when it’s the main keyword

## Common seasonal mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Publishing 1 week before holiday | Follow publish-by table |
| Only seasonal, no evergreen | Keep 70/30 mix |
| Scary horror for ages 4–8 | Spooky-cute instead |
| Trademark holidays characters | Generic symbols only |
| Dating journals to one year | Prefer undated |
| Stuffing all keywords with “Christmas” | Mix evergreen + seasonal |
