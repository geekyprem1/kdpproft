## 3. OFFER SEQUENCING LOGIC (why this order)

- **OTO1 Unlimited first** — always the #1 converter; remove-the-limits is the easiest yes right after purchase.
- **OTO2 DFY** — beginner shortcut; high perceived value while excitement is highest.
- **OTO3 Autopilot** — the emotional "hands-free income" high-ticket.
- **OTO4 Bundle** — cheaper, easy add-on to recover no's on the big tickets.
- **OTO5 Agency** — "make money with it" business angle.
- **OTO6 Reseller** — highest ticket, last, for the serious few.
- **Every OTO has a downsell** — capture the no's at a lower price / lite version.

## 4. PRICING & DIME-SALE STRATEGY

- Start FE at **$17**, dime-sale increment **$0.01–$0.05 per sale**, cap at **$27**.
- Creates real, honest urgency ("price rises with every sale") — a launch staple.
- Keep OTO prices **fixed** (dime-sale only on FE) so affiliates can promote clean numbers.
- Consider a **48-hour "early bird" $12** flash for your own list / top affiliates only.

---

## 5. AFFILIATE STRATEGY (this is where launches are won)

**Reality:** On WarriorPlus/JVZoo, ~80% of sales come from affiliates. Your #1 job pre-launch is recruiting them.

### Commission structure
- **FE: 100%** (yes, give it all away — you profit on OTOs). This is the single biggest affiliate magnet for a $17 front end.
- **OTOs: 50–60%.** This is where you and the affiliate both profit.
- **Recurring/rebills:** none here (all one-time), so lead with the 100% FE + high OTO cut.

### Recruit affiliates 2–3 weeks before launch
1. Build a **JV / Affiliate page** (see §7) with EPC projections, funnel map, swipes, contest.
2. DM/email affiliates who promoted **similar offers** (KDP tools, AI content tools, MMO front ends). Find them on the WarriorPlus/JVZoo leaderboards of comparable launches.
3. Post in **JV Facebook groups** (Warrior JV, JVZoo JV, Muncheye) and list on **Muncheye** (the launch calendar every affiliate watches).
4. Offer **review access** (free copy) to approved affiliates so they can make honest demo videos.

### JV contest (drives leaderboard competition)
- Prize pool e.g. **$2,000–$5,000** split across top 5–10 affiliates.
- Tiered: 1st $1000, 2nd $500, 3rd $300, etc.
- Announce leaderboard daily during launch week to fuel competition.

## 8. LAUNCH WEEK PLAYBOOK

**Best window:** Tuesday–Thursday, **11:00 AM EST** open cart (peak US affiliate activity). Avoid Mondays/Fridays/weekends/holidays.

**Day 1 (Open):** Big email push from you + affiliates. Announce dime-sale. "Doors open."

**Days 2–4 (Momentum):** Daily emails hitting different angles — features, results, objections, bonuses. Post leaderboard updates for affiliates.

**Day 5–6 (Scarcity):** "Price rising fast / early-bird ending." Push the biggest volume here — most sales land in the final 48 hours.

**Day 7 (Close):** "Final hours" emails (2–3 on the last day). Then price jumps to $27 (or cart closes if doing a closed-cart launch).

## 9. EMAIL SWIPES (angles to give affiliates)

Provide 5–7 pre-written emails; each hits a different angle:
1. **Announcement** — "AI writes + designs + formats your Amazon books."
2. **Problem/agitation** — "Why your KDP attempts failed (and the fix)."
3. **Demo** — "Watch 1 keyword become a finished book in 5 min."
4. **Benefit/story** — "From blank screen to catalog without writing a word."
5. **Objection crusher** — "No writing, no design, no rejected files."
6. **Social proof** — early results / testimonials.
7. **Scarcity close** — "Price rising / last hours."

Keep subject lines curiosity + benefit driven. Short paragraphs. One clear CTA/link.

## 10. BONUSES (yours + affiliate-friendly)

**Your bonuses (already in FE copy):** Quickstart guide, 50 niches cheat-sheet, seasonal calendar, keyword swipe file, community.

**Affiliate bonus policy:** Explicitly *encourage* affiliates to add their own bonus stacks — it boosts their conversions and your sales. Provide a "recommended bonus ideas" list so they don't have to think.

## 11. CONVERSION BOOSTERS ON THE PAGES

- **VSL at the top** of FE (video lifts conversions massively on these platforms).
- **Exit-intent popup** on FE with a small discount or bonus reminder.
- **Countdown timer** tied to the dime-sale / price rise.
- **Real testimonials + KDP-accepted screenshots** (swap placeholders before launch).
- **"As featured / built on KDP specs"** trust bar.
- **Order bump** on the FE checkout (e.g. a $9.95 "10 Done-For-You Covers" bump) — easy extra revenue before OTOs even start.

---

## 12. REFUND / CHARGEBACK MINIMIZATION

WarriorPlus/JVZoo watch refund rates — high refunds hurt future launches and affiliate trust.

- **Deliver instantly & clearly** (login + quickstart on the thank-you page).
- **Onboarding email sequence** (day 0, 1, 3, 7) to drive activation — activated users rarely refund.
- **Fast support** (your `/dashboard/support` ticketing is ready).
- **Honest OTO copy** — the DFY/Agency/Reseller "coming soon" features are labeled honestly in the copy to avoid "not as described" refunds.
- **Under-promise on income**, over-deliver on tooling. Keep the earnings disclaimer visible.
- **Don't over-stack OTOs** — 6 + downsells is the sweet spot; more raises refund risk.

## 13. POST-LAUNCH (the long tail)

- **Move to "evergreen"** — after launch week, keep the funnel live at $27 for ongoing organic + affiliate sales.
- **Relaunch on JVZoo** 2–4 weeks later to a fresh crowd.
- **Reward top affiliates** publicly + invite them to promote the evergreen funnel.
- **Email your buyer list** with the OTOs they didn't take (reactivation).
- **Add seasonal promos** (holidays) — perfect fit for a publishing tool.
- **Collect testimonials** from real buyers to strengthen the page for round two.
