# KDP Mafia — Landing Page Demo Video Guide

Ye guide tujhe step-by-step batati hai ki landing page ke liye ek high-converting
demo video kaise banaye — kya search karna hai, kaun sa book banana hai, kaun sa
cover banana hai, aur kya bolna/dikhana hai.

**Target length:** 60–90 seconds (landing page hero video). Isse lamba mat banana —
log 90 sec ke baad drop kar jaate hain.

---

## 1. Demo ka Goal (isko dimaag me rakhna)

Ek hi baat prove karni hai: **"Idea se KDP-ready book + cover tak, minutes me."**

Visitor ko 3 cheezein feel honi chahiye:
1. **Fast** — bahut kaam manually karna nahi padta.
2. **Professional output** — jo bana wo real, publish-ready lag raha hai.
3. **Simple** — koi technical skill nahi chahiye.

Har scene isi ke around hona chahiye. Feature dump mat karna — ek clean journey dikha.

---

## 2. Recording Setup (technical)

**Screen recorder (free options):**
- **OBS Studio** (best, free, Windows) — 1080p/60fps set karna.
- **Windows Game Bar** (Win + G) — quick recording ke liye.
- Ya **Loom** (browser-based, easy) — auto webcam bubble + link.

**Settings:**
- Resolution: **1920x1080 (1080p)**, 30 ya 60 fps.
- Browser ko **full screen / clean** rakh — bookmarks bar hide kar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
- Zoom browser ko 100–110% pe rakh taaki text clearly padhe.
- Ek **incognito/clean window** use kar — koi extension, notification popup na aaye.
- Mouse cursor size thoda bada kar de (Windows Settings > Accessibility > Mouse pointer).

**Audio (optional but recommended):**
- Agar voiceover de raha hai to phone earphones/mic use kar, quiet room me.
- Ya sirf background music + on-screen text captions — voiceover ke bina bhi chalta hai.

**Data ready rakh (IMPORTANT):**
- Demo se pehle account me **credits** hone chahiye (generation ke liye).
- Ek **fresh test account** use kar jisme thodi si history ho (empty bhi chalega,
  onboarding steps achhe dikhte hain).

## 3. Kya Search Karna Hai (Market Intelligence™)

Demo ke liye ek **proven, evergreen, broad-appeal niche** search kar. Aisa niche jo
audience turant samajh jaaye aur "haan ye bikta hai" lage.

**Best demo search (recommended):**
> `large print word search for seniors`

Kyun ye best hai:
- Proven high-demand KDP niche (log jaante hain ye bikta hai).
- Word search ka output **visually clean** hota hai — screen pe achha dikhta hai.
- Broad appeal — koi bhi samajh jaata hai.

**Backup / alternate searches (agar variety chahiye):**
- `christmas activity book for kids` (seasonal, colorful)
- `mindfulness coloring book for adults`
- `sudoku for beginners`
- `dog lovers journal`

**Demo me kya dikhana:**
1. Sidebar me **Market Intelligence™** kholo.
2. Search box me niche type karo (ऊpar wala).
3. **20 scored opportunities** aate hain — demand, competition, evergreen &
   monetization scores ke saath.
4. Ek high-score wale opportunity pe click karke score dikha (2–3 sec).

> Tip: Search se pehle hi ek report generate karke rakh le, taaki demo me loading
> ke liye rukna na pade. Recording me bas "View" karke result dikha de.

---

## 4. Kaun Sa Book Banana Hai (Publishing Studio)

**Recommended: Word Search Book** — demo ke liye best kyunki:
- Generate fast hota hai.
- Output grid-based, clean aur screen pe impressive.
- Us niche (`large print word search for seniors`) se perfectly match karta hai.

**Steps demo me:**
1. Sidebar → **Publishing Studio** (ya direct **Word Search** entry).
2. Topic/title daal: e.g. `Large Print Word Search for Seniors — Volume 1`.
3. Settings quickly dikha (puzzle count, difficulty, large print option).
4. **Generate** dabao → **Production Queue** me job dikhao.
5. Complete hone pe **KDP-ready PDF** ka preview dikhao (interior pages).

**Alternate book types (agar niche change kiya):**
- Christmas niche → **Coloring Book** ya **Activity Book** (colorful, visual).
- Sudoku niche → **Sudoku** generator.
- Journal niche → **Journals & Planners** (Low-Content).

> Demo tip: 2 book types dikha sakta hai (ek puzzle + ek coloring) taaki "20+ book
> types" ka range dikhe — par 60-90 sec me ek hi pe focus karna behtar hai.

---

## 5. Kaun Sa Cover Banana Hai (Cover Studio)

**Recommended cover style: Bold, clean, large-text** — kyunki niche "large print" hai,
cover bhi bold aur readable hona chahiye. Ye demo me professional lagta hai.

**Steps demo me:**
1. Sidebar → **Cover Studio**.
2. Book title + subtitle daal (wahi jo book banaya).
3. Genre/style select kar (clean / bold).
4. **Generate** → AI cover concepts aate hain, genre-aware scoring ke saath.
5. Best-scored cover pick kar, KDP-ready export dikha.

**Cover style recommendation:**
- Simple background + **bada readable title** + ek relevant visual (puzzle grid / senior-friendly theme).
- Zyada busy mat rakhna — clean covers demo me trust build karte hain.

## 8. Editing Tips

- **Speed up boring parts:** typing aur loading ko 1.5x–2x fast kar de (jump cuts).
- **Zoom-in:** jab important cheez ho (score, PDF preview), thoda zoom kar de.
- **Captions/text overlays:** har scene pe ek short caption — mute watchers ke liye zaroori (landing page videos aksar mute chalte hain).
- **Background music:** subtle, upbeat, royalty-free (YouTube Audio Library / Pixabay Music).
- **Cursor highlight:** clicks ko highlight kar (OBS/editing me) taaki eye follow kare.
- **End card:** last frame pe logo + CTA button ("Start Free Trial" / "Get Started").

**Free editing tools:** CapCut (desktop, easy), DaVinci Resolve (advanced free), Clipchamp (Windows built-in).

---

## 9. Do's & Don'ts

**Do:**
- Ek clean, complete journey dikha (research → book → cover → ready).
- Real product use kar — mockup nahi.
- Fast pace rakh, dead time cut kar.
- Captions zaroor daal.

**Don't:**
- Har feature mat dikha (feature dump = confusion).
- Personal email / real customer data screen pe mat aane de.
- 90 sec se lamba mat banana (hero video ke liye).
- Errors/loading spinners lambe mat dikhana — cut kar de.
